Welcome to LogLens

A big data log analytics system for web servers

3.46 million requests to NASA's Kennedy Space Center web server (July–August 1995), stored in Hadoop HDFS, cleaned and analysed with Apache Spark, mined with Spark MLlib and visualised with Apache Superset.

Key takeaways

  • Traffic peaks at 15:00 New York time and on Thursdays; weekends carry about half a weekday's load.
  • 11 of the 12 unusual hours fall on the morning of 13 July 1995, the STS-70 Space Shuttle launch.
  • A Gaussian Mixture Model finds 1,829 automated hosts (6.4% of requests) without any labels.
  • Inside one dataset the linear SVM is the most accurate; trained on one dataset and tested on another, the Factorization Machine transfers best (F1 0.968 vs 0.848).
