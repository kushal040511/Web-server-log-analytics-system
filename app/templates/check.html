How the check works

  1. Normalise the request like the training data: URL-decode, lower-case, keep only the part after ?.
  2. Features: every character, pair and triple of characters, hashed into 3 × 4,096 buckets, plus length, special-character, digit and keyword counts = 12,292 numbers.
  3. Factorization Machine (Spark MLlib): a weight per feature plus a learned interaction between every pair of features, so “' together with or” counts more than either alone.
  4. Decide: probability above 50% = attack.
